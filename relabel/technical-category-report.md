## How the sweep worked

- Every Unsorted video (990 of 991 have captions in the raw batches) was screened on
  title + caption, in English, Italian, Spanish and Portuguese, for: 1v1, 1v2, dribbling,
  ball mastery, first touch, receiving, turns, close control, skill moves.
- 111 videos hit a strong keyword. Each was then read individually: **92 in, 32 rejected**
  (see below). A second pass over weaker signals ("turn", "skill", "technique", "touch"
  in the title) plus a duel/German/French pattern check added 13 more clear fits that the
  strong screen had missed.
- **Strict rule kept:** borderline stayed Unsorted. Nothing was pulled out of the
  existing 15 categories. Exact duplicate posts got identical names on purpose.

### Rejected on review (stayed Unsorted), and why
- **Match/tactical analysis, not drills** — pro-footage breakdowns that merely mention
  1v1, receiving or first touch (most @_coachingsoccer_ and @rcperformancetraining hits,
  Van Dijk / Haaland-vs-Van Dijk explainers, Rodri/Pedri essays, KDB highlight).
- **Promos and recaps** — camp ads, new-player-evaluation ads, "week 1 highlights",
  player praise posts, a Cruyff quote post.
- **Goalkeeper 1v1s** — a GK session built on 1v1 shot-stopping stayed out; that's a
  different discipline from outfield technical work.
- **Too thin to verify** — posts whose caption was only hashtags ("Quality touches",
  "SKILLS TRAINING") with nothing saying what the drill is.

## What's actually in the Unsorted pool (the surprise)

The sweep mostly confirmed the Unsorted pool is **not** a pile of unfiled drills:

- **~298 videos are pure analysis content** (tactics channels — @tifofootball_ alone is
  98, plus @rcperformancetraining, @the.football_analyst, @5amfootball, @_coachingsoccer_).
  They explain the game; they were never drills.
- **~144 are barely soccer at all** — @vmracademy_1 (94) and @realworld_tactical (50)
  are American-football / S&C / athletic-performance accounts, and @azkickingandtraining
  (49) is kicking work. Another ~30 are goalkeeper-specific.
- The real technical-drill content is **concentrated in a handful of accounts**:
  @extrawork.io (24 of the 92 moves — solo ball mastery and dribbling) and
  @fundamentalfutbol (23 — a 1v1 / first-touch specialist) supply half the category
  between them; @ftfssoccer ball mastery adds 12 more.
- Keyword hits alone would have overfilled the category by a third: 32 of 111 strong
  hits were analysis, ads or recaps wearing technical words.

After this sweep the remaining 899 Unsorted are, honestly, mostly analysis, athletic
training, GK work and engagement clips — the drill-shaped content has largely been found.
